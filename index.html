Our compass

Mission

To empower vulnerable communities in Kenya's ASAL counties through sustainable, community-driven interventions that enhance resilience, improve livelihoods, and uphold human dignity — in partnership with local institutions and government.

Vision

A resilient, self-reliant, and prosperous ASAL community where every individual has access to basic needs, opportunities, and a voice in shaping their own future.

Who we are

African Community Aid and Development Initiatives (ACADI) is a locally registered humanitarian and development organisation based in Wajir County, with operational presence across Wajir, Mandera, Garissa, and Marsabit Counties.

We work hand-in-hand with government structures and community leadership to deliver community-driven and accountable interventions in the arid and semi-arid lands (ASALs).

Our approach: local knowledge, participatory planning, and transparency.

  • Community-driven & accountable
  • Collaborative with government & local leaders
  • Focus on most vulnerable populations
  • ASALs expertise – Wajir, Mandera, Garissa, Marsabit
  • Humanitarian principles & neutrality
