Mission

To empower and uplift vulnerable communities in Kenya by promoting equitable access to health, education, livelihoods, and social justice, while championing peace, gender equality, and resilience against climate and economic shocks.

Vision

A self-reliant, peaceful, and prosperous community in Kenya where every person lives in dignity and reaches their full potential.

Who we are

African Community Aid and Development Initiatives (ACADI) is a registered Non-Governmental Organization officially established on September 5, 2025 under the NGO Coordination Board of Kenya.

ACADI was founded by professionals, community leaders, and humanitarian actors who have witnessed firsthand the challenges facing communities in Northern Kenya — from recurring droughts and poor access to essential services, to chronic poverty and insecurity.

We primarily work across Wajir, Mandera, Garissa, Marsabit, Isiolo, Samburu, and Turkana counties, with a long-term goal of expanding across other arid and semi-arid regions of Kenya.
